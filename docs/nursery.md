## HOMESCHOOLING
```
It means counting spoons, sorting socks, and turning your living room into a math lab.

If your toddler or preschooler is at home with you, math can start right now. No fancy kits needed.

Here’s how we teach math to homeschooling kids and keep it playful:

*1. Start with number sense, not numbers*
Before 1+1, play with “more vs less”, “big vs small”, “full vs empty”. Pour water, stack blocks, compare fruits. This builds the foundation for all future math.

*2. Use real life as your textbook*
Counting stairs, setting the table, sharing biscuits equally. When kids see math in daily life, it clicks. For kids going to play to 1st class, this is how you prep them for addition, patterns, and shapes without pressure.

*3. Hands-on > Paper*
Use buttons for counting, Lego for patterns, kitchen items for measuring. Toddlers learn math by touching and moving.

*4. 10 minutes a day is enough*
Short, consistent math games beat 1 hour of forcing. Keep it fun and end before they get bored.

*5. Celebrate mistakes*
Wrong answers teach problem solving. Say “let’s try another way” instead of “no”.

Your child doesn’t need to be a genius. They just need curiosity and you.

```

## Pre-writing

```
*5 pre-writing activities for 1-4 year olds:*

*1. Finger strengthening*: Play dough, tearing paper, squeezing sponges. Fine motor skills for toddlers = grip strength for pencil later.

*2. Big movements first*: Draw in air, sand, atta, shaving foam. Scribbling activities for kids = shoulder + wrist control. Nursery kids need big lines before small letters.

*3. Tongs + tweezers*: Transfer pom poms, rajma, beads. Pincer grip for toddlers = how they’ll hold pencil. Montessori writing prep = practical life.

*4. Tracing lines*: Finger trace roads, mazes, shapes. Pre writing strokes for kids = vertical, horizontal, circles. No pencil needed.

*5. Clothespins + stickers*: Pin to cardboard, peel stickers. Toddler activities at home = finger isolation + focus.

*Working mom tip*: 5 mins during snack time. Skip worksheets till age 4. Strong hands = neat writing later.
```
