Top 25 Angular, TypeScript & JavaScript Interview Questions for Senior Software Engineers & Technical Leads

Preparing for Senior Frontend Developer, Angular Developer, Full Stack Developer, or Technical Lead interviews?

At senior levels, interviewers focus not only on syntax but also on JavaScript internals, Angular architecture, performance optimization, and real-world application design.

Here are 25 important questions worth preparing for 👇

## 🔥 JavaScript – Core Concepts

1️⃣ Explain the Event Loop, Call Stack, Microtasks, and Macrotasks.

2️⃣ What is the difference between "var", "let", and "const"? Explain hoisting and the Temporal Dead Zone.

3️⃣ Explain Closures and their real-world use cases.

4️⃣ What is the difference between Promises, "async/await", and callbacks?

5️⃣ Explain "==" vs "===", shallow copy vs deep copy, and how they affect applications.

6️⃣ What is the difference between Debouncing and Throttling? Where would you use them?

7️⃣ Explain "this", "call()", "apply()", and "bind()".

8️⃣ How does JavaScript handle memory management? What causes memory leaks?

## 🔷 TypeScript – Advanced Concepts

9️⃣ What is the difference between "interface" and "type"?

🔟 Explain Generics and how they improve reusable, type-safe code.

1️⃣1️⃣ What are Union Types, Intersection Types, and Type Guards?

1️⃣2️⃣ Explain "any", "unknown", "never", and "void".

1️⃣3️⃣ What are Utility Types such as "Partial", "Pick", "Omit", and "Record"?

## 🔶 Angular – Architecture & Advanced Concepts

1️⃣5️⃣ What is the difference between Signals, Observables, and Promises?

1️⃣6️⃣ Explain RxJS operators such as "switchMap", "mergeMap", "concatMap", and "exhaustMap".

1️⃣7️⃣ What is the difference between Reactive Forms and Template-Driven Forms?

1️⃣8️⃣ Explain Lazy Loading, Standalone Components, and Angular routing.

2️⃣1️⃣ What is the difference between "ngOnInit", "ngOnChanges", and "ngOnDestroy"?
